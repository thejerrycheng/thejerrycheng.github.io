Place radar b, drive, calibrate

Drag radar b anywhere on the vehicle (or use the sliders), pick the noise, the run length and the kind of motion, then press Drive & calibrate. The drive is simulated at 14 Hz, both ego-velocity streams are generated with the noise you chose, and the paper's solver runs iteration by iteration: closed-form initialisation, then Levenberg–Marquardt with the Schur complement over every time step. The log is the solver's own output.
