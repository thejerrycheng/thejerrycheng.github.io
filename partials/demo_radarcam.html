Mount the radar, offset the clocks, fly, calibrate

Choose where the radar sits on the camera (rotation and lever arm), how late its clock runs (Δt), the monocular scale α the camera is missing, and the radar noise. Press Fly & calibrate: the rig flies the trajectory, the camera stream (30 Hz, scaled poses) and the radar stream (20 Hz ego-velocities, time-shifted) are simulated, then the solver runs live: a Δt grid search with the paper's linear identifiability model as the closed form, then Levenberg–Marquardt over rotation, lever arm, scale and Δt. Drag the 3-D view to orbit.
