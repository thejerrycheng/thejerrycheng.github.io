The pipeline

One scene. Separate responsibilities.

  1. Camera and scale. COLMAP recovers 31 video keyframe poses. Shared landmarks localize the three stills. Metric scale currently assumes a 17 cm cup.
  2. Depth and ownership. Compare Depth Anything V2, DA3 Small and DA3 Large. Fuse multi-view depth, vote on object ownership and remove ambiguous fragments from the fixed room.
  3. Every video frame. SAM 2 processes all 471 decoded frames. The overlay follows each frame’s presentation timestamp, while camera motion is interpolated between reconstructed keyframes.
  4. Appearance and completion. Source images texture independently movable bodies. Unobserved surfaces, clean floor/table plates and smooth table-side illumination are labeled as inferred. Object generators are tested before adoption.
  5. Contact and articulation. MuJoCo uses separate collision geometry, free rigid bodies and an explicitly constrained laptop hinge. Mass and friction remain assumptions.
  6. Validation. Check topology, object movement, source-view fit, mask alignment, contact under torque and portable simulation packages. Preserve unsuccessful experiments.

Selected repair

Keep the surface.
Close the object.

The chair repair preserves the observed fronts and closes their backs. Multi-view carving completes the backpack. Voxel union and local repair remove the self-intersections that a simple watertight test missed. Every selected part moves with its object.

Object completion

Closed is not the same as correct.

TripoSR generated complete chair and backpack candidates from the actual video masks on CPU. Similarity fitting tested 24 orientation initializations against the reconstructed surfaces. The candidates have lower silhouette agreement than the partial scans and are excluded from the selected scene.

Research direction

A visual world with physical objects.

RE3Sim and SplatSim inform the separation of photographic rendering and physical geometry. SAM 3D Objects and TRELLIS 2 are candidates for complete object assets.

SAM 3D code and converted weights are downloaded. Its Apple-silicon port cannot access Metal in the current execution session; the official CUDA path and TRELLIS have not run. Installed software is not counted as a successful experiment.

The harness records commands, configuration hashes, outputs and failures. New captures require their own landmarks, segmentation prompts, scale measurements and fitted geometry. Robot-frame calibration and real contact validation remain required before policy transfer.
