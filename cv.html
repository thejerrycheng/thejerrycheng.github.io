M2 Read the Room — Cooperative Multi-Robot Transport Lead researcher — grasp-aware planning and learned local control Independent research · New York 2026 – present
  • Two mobile manipulators carry one load. The planner searches over the grasped assembly rather than the payload alone: in the illustrated beam geometry, admissible yaw through the aperture narrows from 25.8° to 5.5° once the carrier footprints are counted.
  • A 10 Hz object-level MPC follows measured path progress; one frozen 50 Hz actor — the same weights on both robots — turns shared references and local observations into 21 actions per robot.
  • Frozen cohort: 35 / 40 full missions across ten scenes, four seeds each, acquisition included. All five failures stop before verified-route transport. Aligning the collision representation and margin moved a paired eight-scene comparison from 36/40 to 40/40.
  • An instrumented panel trial shows one hand at 363 N and another at 107 N·m — opposing reactions that robot-level totals hide entirely.

Startups

Motion Picture Robotics

Cinema robotics • 2025–present

Film-grade robotic camera motion is astonishing — and priced out of reach, with professional camera-motion rigs running well into six figures. I'm building a mobile cinema robot arm that flips that equation: portable enough to carry onto any set, affordable enough for independent creators, and simple enough to run without a dedicated technical crew. The aim is to put programmable, repeatable, cinematic camera moves in everyone's hands.
