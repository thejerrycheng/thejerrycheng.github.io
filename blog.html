NEO X1 (1X): Human-in-the-Loop Today, Autonomy Tomorrow

Nov 2025 • Robotics, Control

1X announced NEO, a consumer-facing humanoid aimed at real homes rather than labs. The notable choice here isn’t a flashy demo—it’s the product strategy: start with human-in-the-loop operation (scheduled expert tele-assists and remote piloting) to safely cover the long tail of household edge cases, then ratchet up autonomy as the data and policies improve.

  • Why human-in-the-loop? Homes are adversarial: pets, cables, soft objects, reflective surfaces, toys. Expert-guided sessions both “get the job done now” and create high-value demonstrations for future autonomy (think: DAGGER-like data with strong safety guardrails). :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
  • Hardware snapshot. ~5′6″, ~66 lb; lift ≈154 lb, carry ≈55 lb; tendon drives, soft 3D-lattice suit, ~22 dB operation; 4-hour runtime, self-charging. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Compute & I/O. Nvidia-class onboard compute (Jetson Thor-based), dual 8.85 MP fisheye, 4-mic beamforming, Wi-Fi/5G. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Pricing. $20,000 ownership (Early Access) or $499/mo subscription, $200 refundable deposit, US deliveries starting 2026. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

The honest catch: this is not zero-trust, fully autonomous out of the box. You’ll allow scheduled expert guidance and accept limits (no outdoor use, water-sensitive body, child/pet cautions). In exchange, you get a system that actually completes chores and steadily improves—an approach that trades bravado for learning velocity. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
