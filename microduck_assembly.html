Flight controller T3 / R3 / GNDDuck computer pin 16 / 18 / 203 wires, 3.3 V serial. T3 → RX, R3 → TX.
Radio receiverFlight controller T2 / R2Arm and kill switch stay on the radio.

The duck computer is a Radxa ZERO 3W; pins 16 and 18 are its UART4. Check that the robot HAT leaves them free, or use a USB-C cable from the flight controller instead. No 5 V wire between the boards: ground only. Not bench-tested.
