Abstract

What is IRIS?

We present IRIS (Intelligent Robotic Imaging System), a low-cost, 3D-printed 6-DOF cinema robot arm that learns cinematic camera motions from human demonstrations via goal-conditioned visuomotor imitation learning. Traditional motion-control cinema systems cost $100K–$250K and require trained operators. IRIS achieves comparable motion quality at ~$992 in materials, with a 1.5 kg payload capacity and ~1 mm repeatability, by combining quasi-direct-drive Unitree GO-M8010-6 actuators with a 200 Hz RS-485 ROS Noetic control stack. An operator physically guides the arm to collect demonstrations; these are then used to train an ACT-inspired CVAE transformer that predicts sequences of 6-DOF joint actions from RGB images and a goal frame. Our best model achieves 46.2% task success and 97% of human visual alignment (0.847 vs. 0.874), while being 6× smoother than human operators (jerk: 0.61 vs. 3.64 m/s³). All hardware designs, firmware, simulation, and training pipelines are fully open-sourced.

Key Contributions
🔩
Open-Source Cinema Robot Hardware
Sub-$1K 6-DOF arm with quasi-direct-drive Unitree GO-M8010-6 actuators, 1.5 kg payload, ~1 mm repeatability. Full STEP files, BOM, and mechanical docs released.
🧪
High-Fidelity MuJoCo Simulation
Physics digital twin with analytical FK/IK, RRT* and potential-field planners, and cinema shot modes (Crane, Dolly, Pan) — DH-consistent with real hardware for safe sim-to-real transfer.
🧠
Goal-Conditioned Visuomotor IL
CVAE action chunking transformer trained on kinesthetic demonstrations, with ResNet18 backbone, Spatial Softmax, and 9 ablation variants across architectures and input modalities.
💻
Full ROS Noetic Integration
200 Hz RS-485 hardware driver, joint calibration, sim-to-real live mirroring, and kinesthetic teach-and-repeat workflow with gravity compensation.

Results

97% of expert alignment. 6× smoother.

CVAE Full achieves 46.2% task success and near-human visual alignment (0.847 vs. 0.874), while producing significantly smoother motion than human demonstrations.

Method N Success Rate ↑ Visual Align. ↑ Avg Jerk (m/s³) ↓ SRR ↑
Expert (Human) 10 90.0% 0.874 3.64 67.1%
CVAE Full ⭐ 13 46.2% 0.847 0.61 32.7%
Incremental (ablation) 6 0.0% 0.636 0.83 35.2%
RGB Only 3 0.0% 0.584 1.65 7.2%
Visual (no joints) 4 0.0% 0.536 1.59 7.3%
