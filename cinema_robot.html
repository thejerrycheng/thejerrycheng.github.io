Abstract

What is IRIS?

We present IRIS (Intelligent Robotic Imaging System), an open-source 6-degree-of-freedom cinema robot arm that makes professional motion-control cinematography accessible to researchers, indie filmmakers, and hardware builders. Traditional cinema robots cost $100K–$250K and require trained operators. IRIS achieves comparable motion quality at under $1,000 in materials through quasi-direct-drive Unitree GO-M8010-6 actuators, a custom RS-485 control stack running at 200 Hz, and a full ROS Noetic framework. A high-fidelity MuJoCo simulation environment with analytical FK/IK and RRT* motion planners enables safe trajectory design before real deployment. To close the loop toward autonomous shot composition, we train visuomotor imitation learning policies from kinesthetic demonstrations, evaluating three architectures: a CVAE-based action chunking model, a deterministic transformer, and a CNN-BC baseline. All hardware designs, firmware, simulation, and training pipelines are open-sourced.

Key Contributions
🔩
Open-Source Cinema Robot Hardware
Sub-$1K 6-DOF arm with Unitree GO-M8010-6 actuators. Full STEP files, BOM, and mechanical docs released.
🧪
High-Fidelity MuJoCo Simulation
Physics simulation with analytical FK/IK, RRT* planning, and DH-parameter consistency with real hardware.
🧠
Visuomotor Imitation Learning
CVAE action chunking, deterministic transformer, and CNN-BC baseline trained from RGB-D kinesthetic demonstrations.
💻
Full ROS Noetic Integration
200 Hz RS-485 hardware driver, joint calibration, sim-to-real sync, and kinesthetic teach-and-repeat workflow.
