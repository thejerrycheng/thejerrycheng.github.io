Meet IRIS,
Cinema-Grade Motion Control for Everyone

IRIS brings cinema-grade precision, high-speed movement, and intuitive control to creators at a fraction of the cost.

A Cinema Robot, Reimagined.

Cinema robots come from industrial automation — massive robotic arms adapted for cameras. They deliver movements impossible for humans: ultra-smooth arcs, sub-millimeter precision, and repeatable paths for VFX and product shoots.

IRIS brings that capability to every creator.

Cinema Robots Were Never Designed for Creators.

Traditional motion-control robots were built for factories, not film sets. They’re massive, expensive, and require trained operators. Great for big studios — inaccessible for everyone else.

We Rebuilt the Cinema Robot from Scratch.

Instead of shrinking industrial hardware, IRIS is a clean-sheet redesign — custom mechanics, lightweight structure, and intelligent motion software. Everything optimized for shooting, not manufacturing.

Smooth. Fast. Repeatable.

IRIS delivers motion that feels alive — ultra-smooth paths, rapid acceleration, and repeatability for precision takes. Perfect for product shots, slow-motion arcs, and high-speed creative work.

Control, Reimagined.

No industrial UI. No training. Just a modern app made for creators — adjust keyframes, preview paths, and run shots with a tap.

Built for Everything You Shoot.

IRIS is fast enough for action, precise enough for VFX, and compact enough for apartments, studios, and small sets. It goes wherever your creativity goes.

Compact by Design.

IRIS fits into your workflow — lightweight, portable, and designed for creators with limited space. No rigging. No setup crew.
