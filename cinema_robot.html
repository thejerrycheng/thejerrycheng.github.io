Meet IRIS,
Cinema-Grade Motion Control for Everyone

IRIS brings cinema-grade precision, high-speed movement, and intuitive control to creators at a fraction of the cost.

A Cinema Robot, Reimagined.

Cinema robots traditionally come from industrial automation — massive robotic arms adapted to carry high-speed cinema cameras. They deliver movements impossible for humans: sub-millimeter repeatability, ultra-fast arcs, and perfectly repeatable paths for VFX and product shoots.

IRIS brings that capability to every creator.

Why Cinema Robots Cost $100,000+

Systems like Bolt or KUKA combine industrial-grade actuators, multi-axis control systems, and proprietary motion software — requiring trained operators and massive budgets.

IRIS eliminates these barriers through a complete redesign of hardware, firmware, and motion-intelligent software.

Designed with Three Objectives.

IRIS is built on a full mechanical, electrical, and algorithmic redesign aimed at bringing cinematic motion control to everyday creators.

1. Make cinema robotics affordable.

Traditional systems cost $100k–$250k. IRIS reduces this by over 100× using custom actuators, optimized 3D-printed structures, and simplified electronics — targeting a sub-$1,000 system.

2. Deliver true cinematic performance.

IRIS maintains key traits of cinema robots: high-speed motion, precise keyframing, and repeatable paths suitable for product cinematography and slow-motion commercial shoots.

3. Make it intuitive and learning-free.

Instead of industrial interfaces, IRIS provides a modern UI designed for creators — no robotics background needed.
