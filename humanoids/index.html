About

A personal guide to the humanoid era

This is a personal, liquid-glass field guide to the world's humanoid robots — the machines, the companies building them, how much they've raised, who founded them and where they studied, and the specs that matter.

Credit where it's due

The robot roster, photos, and skill scores are drawn from humanoid.guide, an excellent and continuously-updated catalog of the humanoid landscape. Thank you for assembling it. This site is a fan-made viewer — please visit and support the original.

The funding & founder data is researched

humanoid.guide lists the robots and specs; it does not publish company funding, founders, or their universities. Those fields here were researched and cross-checked from public sources (company sites, Crunchbase, press, news) by an automated research pass built with Claude. It's a fast-moving, often-opaque industry: some figures are undisclosed, and anything that couldn't be verified is left blank rather than guessed. Treat funding numbers as best-effort estimates, not gospel.
