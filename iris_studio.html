How to use it

Shots: click a preset; it plays and loops. space pauses, the scrubber seeks. Joints: sliders. Task space: drag the yellow gizmo on the camera, or use the jog buttons. Lens: focal length, focus, aperture. Record & train: collect demos, train, run.

Views: 1 studio, 2 monitor, 3 film. r record, h home.

Kinematics: the MuJoCo model of the thesis (six hinge joints, the same body tree). Camera: Sony α7R III body, 16–150 mm servo zoom, thin-lens depth of field. Products: CC0 models from Poly Haven and procedural props. Nothing here is a physics simulation: the arm follows the planned joint path with a 1.5 rad/s per-joint cap.
