Each capture here is a separate single-image prediction, not a jointly optimized multi-view reconstruction. The source camera is the strongest appearance check. Large viewpoint changes can expose warped or missing geometry. Cup movement is a segmented appearance preview; collisions and policy training use the separate MuJoCo assets.

The model runs offline on CPU. See the source-view image measurements and viewer checks. The browser renders the predicted splats with Spark. This comparison does not substitute for camera calibration, contact validation or held-out real photographs.

Appearance

Gaussian splats preserve fine image detail without requiring a triangle mesh for every visible structure. Their geometry is a learned single-view hypothesis.

Interaction

The cup’s visible splats are separated from the background. Unobserved surfaces revealed by moving it are not measured. The hybrid scene remains the simulation asset.
