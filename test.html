Introduction

Mundane, made magical.

Lumo is powered by modern robotics and large-scale learning, turning everyday chores into something you never have to think about.

The idea

Robots that give you time back.

We believe home robots should feel invisible most of the time: quietly moving around your space, picking things up, tidying after meals, and resetting the room for the next day.

Services

We start with repetitive chores.

Clearing dishes, handling utensils, carrying cups, sorting socks, wiping crumbs, and more. We focus on a few high-impact chores and make them work in real, messy homes.

Technology

From imitation to intuition.

Lumo learns from dense human motion data, distilled into a compact model that runs fully on the robot.

Skill capture

Teach Lumo like you’d teach a person.

Instead of teleoperating a robot in your home, we use wearable devices to record how people naturally move and manipulate objects. Those movements are turned into reusable skills for Lumo.

Onboard intelligence

Fast decisions, right where they happen.

Motion planning, perception, and control all run on Lumo without streaming video to the cloud. That means lower latency and better privacy by design.

Learning loop

More homes, better skills.

Our early households send back anonymized motion data that help Lumo generalize across layouts, lighting, and clutter. Every new environment makes the system more robust.

Design

Designed for homes, not labs.

Soft edges, calm motion, and a footprint that fits next to your furniture — Lumo is meant to live where you live.

Safety

Low center of gravity, soft shell.

A stable wheelbase and compliant actuators help Lumo stay balanced and gentle to the touch, even when carrying delicate items.

Aesthetic

Tidy, neutral, and customizable.

Choose between multiple finishes and accent colors to blend Lumo into your space instead of making it the centerpiece.

Everyday care

Easy to wipe, easy to maintain.

The outer shell is designed to be cleaned with common household products. No exposed screws, no sharp corners, and no complex routines.

FAQs

Answers to common questions.

We’re starting with a small number of homes and expanding as we learn.

What can Lumo do today? ›

Lumo can clear tables, carry dishes, load compatible dishwashers, and handle small laundry items like socks. We’re steadily adding more skills as our test homes use the system.

When will Lumo be available to buy? ›

We’re currently in a limited beta with selected households. We expect to share pricing and availability after we complete this beta and are confident in long-term reliability.

Does Lumo send video to the cloud? ›

No. Lumo processes visual data on-device. We use separate opt-in data collection channels to improve our models while protecting your everyday privacy.

How big is Lumo? ›

Lumo is roughly the height of a side table in its resting posture, and can reach up to cabinet height when needed. Exact dimensions will be published closer to launch.
