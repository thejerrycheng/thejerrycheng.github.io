Jerry (Qilong) Cheng 「程启龙」

I’m currently a Robotics Ph.D. Candidate at NYU working on RL for humanoid locomotion and dexterous manipulation, with prior work in state estimation (STARS Lab, Prof. Jonathan Kelly), haptics/HRI (DGP Lab, Prof. Daniel Wigdor), and learning-driven manipulation/control (UofT Robotics Institute, Prof. Matthew Mackay; Prof. Ali Bereyhi).

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing unified policies that integrate visual perception, loco-manipulation, and dexterous manipulation for robotic systems. I also a strong believer of a quote from Steve Jobs that “Those who are truly serious about software should design their own hardware.”
