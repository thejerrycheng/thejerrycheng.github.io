Jerry (Qilong) Cheng 「程启龙」

I’m Jerry (Qilong) Cheng, a Robotics Ph.D. Candidate building the next generation of Embodied Intelligence. My mission is to engineer generalized, highly articulate robots for the home—vertically integrated systems designed to automate daily tasks, save us time, and seamlessly enrich our lives.

Research Vision

I specialize in robot learning and embodied control, unifying reinforcement learning and foundation models to train robust visuos-motor policies directly on real-world data for mobile dexterous manipulation.

• Dexterous Loco-Manipulation: Fusing foundational models with whole-body control for fluid, long-horizon mobile manipulation in human environments.

• Low-Level Motor Cortex: Developing precise, torque-level RL policies to bridge semantic high-level reasoning and fine-grained actuation.

• Scalable Data Pipelines: Building spatial computing tools and custom kinematic/dynamics interfaces to capture high-fidelity human demonstrations.

• Full-Stack Co-Design: Architecting vertically integrated dexterous UMI platforms that mitigate embodiment gaps.

I am also a strong believer in Steve Jobs’ idea that “those who are truly serious about software should design their own hardware.”
