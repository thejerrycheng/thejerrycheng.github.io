Timeline

The important papers on one vertical spine, newest at the top, each tagged with the methodology it introduced or exemplifies. Pick a methodology to follow just that lineage down the years.

The network

Papers as nodes, intellectual lineage as edges. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, drag a node to pull the neighbourhood apart, click to open it. Colour is the tree; size is how load-bearing the paper is.
