Design Notes from Building a Low-Cost Cinema Robot Arm

Oct 2025 • Hardware, Mechatronics

Why differential wrist?

It lets two motors share load across pitch/roll, reducing motor size at the end effector and improving dynamic response for fast pans/tilts.

FOC choices

  • Low-kv motors + high pole count → smoother low-speed control for slow cinematic moves.
  • Current sensing close to the phase → better torque estimation and quieter operation.

Carbon vs aluminum

  • Carbon links cut weight and inertia at the cost of more complex joints and bonding.
  • Aluminum is forgiving for rapid iteration and surviving “oops” moments.

Supply chain notes

Order motor/gearbox batches with 10–15% buffer, and standardize connectors/fasteners early—your future self will thank you.
