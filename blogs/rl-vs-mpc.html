RL vs MPC for Humanoid Locomotion — Practical Tradeoffs

Nov 2025 • Control, Robotics

Quick take. WBC-QP/CLF-QP are excellent myopic trackers that enforce contact and torque limits but don’t natively pick contact modes. MPC, especially contact-implicit MPC, can discover/select modes and plan several steps ahead—but at a compute and modeling cost. Residual RL glues them: MPC gives stable priors; RL adapts contacts and nuisances.

When I reach for MPC

  • Contact sequencing matters (stepping locations/timing) and horizons of 0.3–1.0s help.
  • Model is decent; I can afford RTI or sampling + warm starts.

When I reach for RL

  • Messy terrain, frictional uncertainty, or frequent impacts.
  • We can generate data in sim and need robustness over elegance.

Best of both

  • MPC for global intent + feasibility; residual RL for contact finesse and disturbance rejection.
  • Safety layer: CLF-QP or projection to keep torques/COP/GRFs feasible.
