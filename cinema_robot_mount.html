Before you move

Tell the robotLoad: 1.43 kg. Centre of mass 82 mm out from the flange.
Thread check4 to 5 mm of the camera screw above the shelf. More than 5.5 mm damages the camera.
Tighten againAfter a day, and after 100 hours. PLA settles.
HeatPLA softens near 55 °C. Stop if the flange is hot to hold.
