Actuator System

Unitree GO-M8010-6

IRIS uses six Unitree GO-M8010-6 quasi-direct-drive motors across all joints. The low gear ratio enables compliant, backdrivable motion — critical for kinesthetic teaching and safe interaction.

Mechanical Structure

Lightweight by Design

🔗 Structural Members

Carbon fiber composite tubes and CNC-machined aluminum brackets balance stiffness with weight. The arm is designed to be modular — joints and links can be replaced independently.

⚖ Weight Budget

Total arm weight: ~4.2 kg (without camera). Designed for desk-mount operation on a standard tripod plate or 15 mm rail system.

Quick Assembly Notes

While the full guide is being written, here are key points for experienced builders:

  • Zero all motors before mechanical assembly using the calibration script.
  • Tighten all M3 joint screws to 1.2 N·m (no Loctite — motors need occasional repositioning).
  • The differential wrist (joints 5 & 6) requires special attention — see calibrate_joint_states.py.
  • RS-485 termination resistor (120 Ω) goes on the last motor in the chain.
  • Ground all motor housings to chassis — important for RS-485 signal integrity.
